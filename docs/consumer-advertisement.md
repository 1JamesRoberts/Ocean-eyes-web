# OceanEyes

## AI-Powered Aquarium Monitoring. Zero Guesswork.

OceanEyes is a smart aquarium monitoring system that combines real-time water chemistry telemetry with on-device computer vision. It counts your fish, classifies species, tracks water clarity, and alerts you the moment something changes — all from a single dashboard.

---

## See What Your Tank Sees

Built-in ONNX models run fish detection, species classification, and turbidity analysis directly on the stream. No cloud uploads. No latency. Just frame-by-frame intelligence.

- **24+ species recognized** — from Neon Tetras to Discus, with confidence-scored classification
- **Live fish counting** with bounding-box overlays on the video feed
- **Turbidity scoring** (FNU) to catch cloudiness before it becomes a problem

## Real-Time Water Chemistry

Track the metrics that matter. The dashboard surfaces pH, temperature, ammonia, nitrite, and water clarity in a single health index scored 0–10.

- Instant-read gauges with threshold-aware color coding
- Trend graphs for every parameter
- Automatic baseline comparison after water changes

## Live Video, Calibrated to Your Tank

Connect any webcam or RTSP stream. Set the water line once and OceanEyes ignores reflections, bubbles, and substrate — focusing only on the aquatic volume.

- Fullscreen inventory mode with AI overlay
- Snapshot gallery for manual review
- Stream adjustments (contrast, brightness, temperature) for low-light tanks

## Alerts That Actually Matter

Get notified when parameters drift or fish counts change unexpectedly. Each alert includes before/after diagnostics and a recommended action.

- pH spikes, temperature swings, clarity drops
- Missing fish or unexpected newcomers
- Configurable thresholds per tank

## A Dashboard Worth Looking At

OceanEyes runs as a responsive web app with a premium glassmorphism UI, teal-cyan accent palette, and full dark mode support. It looks as clean as the water it monitors.

---

## Get Started

1. Mount the OceanEyes camera unit above your tank.
2. Scan the QR code on the device to link it to your dashboard.
3. Watch the AI initialize and start monitoring in under 60 seconds.
