Core application roles

How the palette appears in the product

Brand and actions

Pine Teal anchors primary actions and selected states; Verdigris lifts gradients and active controls.

Surfaces and structure

White and Azure Mist establish the light glass system, page backgrounds, hover fills, and borders.

Data and focus

The bright aquatic spectrum carries charts, focus feedback, highlights, and informational measurements.
