Core application roles

How the palette appears in the product

Brand and actions

Prussian Blue anchors primary actions with White text. Verdigris and Turquoise carry focus, selection, and active states.

Surfaces and structure

Azure Mist is the canvas; White supplies solid and glass surfaces; Pearl Aqua and Tropical Teal define borders and chart grids.

Data and focus

Prussian Blue and Verdigris carry readable data marks; Dark Cyan identifies active navigation icons; Turquoise and Neon Ice support accents and highlights.

Source of truth: src/index.css. This document records the application’s light-mode palette. Transparent tints may only derive from these ten primitives. Status colors, Google branding, species identity, camera calibration, recording, and detection colors remain intentional exceptions when their hue carries functional meaning. Use Prussian Blue on light surfaces and White on Prussian Blue; do not place normal-size White text on the brighter aquatic colors.
