# OceanEyes

## Your Aquarium, Under a Watchful Eye

You love your fish. But you can't stare at the tank all day. OceanEyes keeps an eye on things for you — tracking water quality, counting your fish, and letting you know if something needs attention. It's like having an experienced aquarist check in on your tank, every minute of every day.

---

## Know Your Fish Are Doing Fine

A smart camera watches your tank and recognizes the fish inside it. You can check in anytime from your phone or computer and see exactly who's swimming around.

- **Knows dozens of popular fish** — from colorful Neon Tetras to elegant Angelfish and vibrant Guppies
- **Counts your fish automatically** so you know if someone's hiding or missing
- **Shows you a live view** whenever you want to check in

## Catch Water Problems Before They Get Serious

OceanEyes measures the stuff that matters — pH, temperature, ammonia, and how clear your water is. If something drifts out of the safe zone, you'll know right away.

- Simple color-coded scores so you know everything's okay at a glance
- Easy-to-read graphs that show trends over time
- One overall "tank health" score from 0 to 10

## Beautiful and Easy to Use

The dashboard looks great on any device — phone, tablet, or computer. It has a clean, modern design with a calming ocean-inspired look and a dark mode for late-night check-ins.

- Works on any screen size
- Dark mode for easy viewing day or night
- Clean, uncluttered layout — no confusing menus

## Alerts That Help, Not Hassle

If your heater fails, your pH crashes, or your water gets cloudy, OceanEyes sends you a clear alert with a simple explanation of what's happening and what to do about it.

- Temperature swings, pH spikes, and cloudy water
- Missing fish or unexpected new arrivals
- Customizable sensitivity so you only get alerts that matter

---

## Get Started in Minutes

1. Place the OceanEyes camera above your tank.
2. Scan the QR code on the device with your phone.
3. That's it. You're monitoring.
