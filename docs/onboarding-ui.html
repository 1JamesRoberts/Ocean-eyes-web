Front-facing / external

Place your camera on a stand or the floor in front of the tank, angled up toward the front glass.

Better for

  • Equipment status checks
  • Fish position and movement
  • Reading gauges and displays

Mount your camera on the right-hand side glass panel so it looks inward across the tank.

Better for

  • Fish health monitoring
  • Behavior and schooling patterns
  • Simple, discreet mounting
